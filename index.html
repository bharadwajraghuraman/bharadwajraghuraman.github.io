About Me

I am a PhD researcher at ETH Zurich working on the system-level optimisation of electric drivetrains, with a focus on power electronics, multilevel inverters, and electric machines.

My research focuses on developing efficient and reliable next-generation electric drivetrain systems using advanced power semiconductor technologies and modulation techniques.

Research

Multilevel Inverters

Four-level Flying Capacitor inverters and their application in high-voltage electric drivetrains.

PWM & Modulation

Optimisation of PWM schemes and voltage distribution for improved drivetrain efficiency.

Electric Machines

Modelling and optimisation of electric machine losses for system-level drivetrain optimisation.

EMI & Bearing Currents

Investigation of electromagnetic interference and common-mode currents in electric drive systems.
