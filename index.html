Bharadwaj Raghuraman

Power Electronics & Electric Drivetrains

I am a PhD researcher at ETH Zurich, working on advanced power electronic converters and electric drive systems. My research focuses on multilevel inverters, modulation strategies, loss optimisation and the interaction between power converters and electrical machines.

Research Publications

About Me

I am currently pursuing a PhD at the High Power Electronics Laboratory, ETH Zurich, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Jürgen Biela.

My research lies at the intersection of power electronics and electric machine design, with a particular interest in high-performance electric vehicle drivetrains. I am interested in developing converter topologies and control strategies that improve system-level efficiency, power density and electromagnetic compatibility.

Before joining ETH Zurich, I worked in the automotive and aerospace industries, contributing to electric machine design and optimisation for applications ranging from passenger vehicles to high-performance and aerospace electric propulsion systems.

Research Interests

Multilevel Inverters

Design and optimisation of multilevel power converters, particularly flying capacitor inverter topologies for high-voltage EV drivetrains.

PWM & Modulation

Advanced modulation strategies, switching sequence optimisation, capacitor voltage balancing and loss-aware PWM.

Electric Machines

Electromagnetic design, efficiency optimisation and system-level analysis of electric machines for automotive applications.

EMI & Bearing Currents

Common-mode and differential-mode noise modelling, electromagnetic compatibility and bearing current analysis in electric drives.
